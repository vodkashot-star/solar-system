# Celestial Features Reference

Parameters and their significance in astronomical classification.

## Stellar Parameters

### Temperature
- **Range**: 2,000K - 50,000K
- **Classification**: 
  - M-type: < 3,500K (red)
  - K-type: 3,500-5,000K (orange)
  - G-type: 5,000-6,000K (yellow, like Sun)
  - F-type: 6,000-7,500K (yellow-white)
  - A-type: 7,500-10,000K (white)
  - B-type: 10,000-30,000K (blue-white)
  - O-type: > 30,000K (blue)

### Luminosity
- **Range**: 0.0001 - 1,000,000 L☉ (solar luminosities)
- **Significance**: Determines star's position on H-R diagram

### Mass
- **Range**: 0.08 - 100+ M☉ (solar masses)
- **Significance**: Dictates lifecycle and fate

### Radius
- **Range**: 0.01 - 1000+ R☉ (solar radii)
- **Significance**: Correlates with temperature/luminosity

## Planetary Parameters

### Orbital Period
- **Range**: Hours to thousands of years
- **Significance**: Determines year length and climate zones

### Semi-Major Axis
- **Range**: 0.01 - 100+ AU
- **Significance**: Average distance from star

### Eccentricity
- **Range**: 0 (circle) to < 1 (ellipse)
- **Significance**: Orbit shape affects seasonal variation

### Axial Tilt
- **Range**: 0° - 180°
- **Significance**: Causes seasons (Earth: 23.5°)

## Galaxy Parameters

### Redshift
- **Range**: -0.001 (blue shift) to > 10
- **Significance**: Measures recession velocity/distance

### Star Formation Rate
- **Range**: 0 - 1000+ M☉/year
- **Significance**: Activity level (Milky Way: ~1-2 M☉/year)
